# Ajedrez y Computación

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students learning chess algorithms and artificial intelligence. The user confirmed that students and navigation between chapters take priority. The existing book also welcomes curious readers without specialist programming or chess knowledge.

## Product Purpose

A free bilingual web edition of Alejandro Fernández Camello’s book, connecting chess problems to search algorithms, machine learning and reinforcement learning.

## Operating Context

Readers explore the table of contents, read chapters in Spanish or English, search concepts, experiment with lesson figures and resume reading on the same device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve all existing content, routes, chapter order, mathematical notation, figures, interactive lessons, reading progress, search, language switching and light/dark themes. Built with Astro and Bun. Existing deployment targets Vercel and the production domain recorded in astro.config.mjs.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the book title and author. The user explicitly requests a complete visual redesign with exceptional personality, replacing the incumbent look. The user authorizes publication after completion. The user likes the new design and rejects ornamental divider lines without functional meaning and decorative periods on titles/subtitles.

## Evidence on Hand

Spanish and English Markdown chapters in src/content; structured chapter manifests; original book illustrations and diagrams in public/assets/book; lesson models and existing reading controls. The printed Spanish book is linked on Amazon. Do not invent claims or alter the educational text.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the source material.
- Make finding and moving between chapters effortless.
- Support focused study and readable mathematical content.
- Give both languages the same functional and visual quality.
